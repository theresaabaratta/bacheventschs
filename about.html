About Bach Events CHS

A Charleston-only booking site for bachelorette and bachelor groups.

Bach Events CHS covers one city: Charleston, South Carolina. We list party boats, private charters, bar and food tours, group activities, entertainment, and dated events from businesses that operate in the Charleston area, from downtown and the French Quarter to Folly Beach, Mount Pleasant, Shem Creek, and Isle of Palms.

National booking platforms list thousands of cities and treat Charleston as one more page. We are local, small, and focused. Every listing on this site is a real Charleston business with a working booking link, and we check them by hand.

How listings are chosen

We list experiences that bachelorette and bachelor groups actually book: group-friendly, bookable in advance, and run by an established local operator. Before a listing goes up, we confirm the booking link works and shows live availability. Listings that stop taking bookings come down.

How booking works

When you click a booking button, the operator's own calendar opens on this site. Most operators use FareHarbor, a booking system used by tour and activity companies, so you see real-time availability and pay through a secure checkout. Your confirmation, tickets, and any changes come directly from the operator. Some vendors take bookings through their own website, and those listings link there instead.

How we make money

Bach Events CHS may earn a commission when you book through some links on this site. It does not change the price you pay. Vendors may also pay for placement; paid placements are labeled.

Contact

Questions, corrections, or listing requests: admin@bacheventschs.com. Vendors can request a listing.
