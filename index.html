Planning

Planning a bach weekend in Charleston

Most groups build the weekend around one big water day. Folly Beach groups book the Charleston SUP Safaris "Lady Liz" charter for a smaller crew, while larger parties lean toward Tiki Tours of Charleston or the BYOB Saltwater Cycle Brew Barge, which seats up to 18. Carolina Cabana Cruises runs a floating-cabana option out of the Ashley River, and Sip and Sea Charleston builds its party boat specifically for bachelorette and bachelor groups.

On land, the downtown Charleston Culinary Tours pub walk covers the French Quarter with drinks included, and Woodchuckers axe throwing is an easy, inexpensive add-on for a Friday afternoon. For the night in at your rental, Michael At Your Place brings private entertainment to you.

Weekend boat slots from spring through fall go first, so lock in the water day before anything else. Most operators require the full group count at booking, and many charters have a 24-hour to 7-day cancellation window, so check the terms in checkout before you pay.
